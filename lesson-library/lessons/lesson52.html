Grammar Time!

Grammatika
like / love / enjoy + -ing (I like playing games)
'like/love/enjoy' dan keyin fe'l -ing oladi
I like playing chess.Men shaxmat o'ynashni yaxshi ko'raman.
She loves listening to music.U musiqa tinglashni yaxshi ko'radi.
He enjoys walking the dog.U itni sayr qildirishni yoqtiradi.
❌
Wrong: *I like play games.*
Right: *I like playing games.*

  1. I like ___ (swim) in summer.
  2. She loves ___ (listen) to music.
  3. He enjoys ___ (play) chess.
  4. We like ___ (watch) movies.

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 My Free Time

In my free time I like doing different things. I love reading comics and drawing pictures. On sunny days, I ride my bike with my cousin. I also enjoy playing chess with my father — but he always wins! In the evening, I like watching movies with my family. Sometimes we listen to music and sing together. What do you like doing in your free time?

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What does the writer love doing?
  2. Who does he ride his bike with?
  3. Why does he like playing chess?
  4. What does the family do in the evening?
