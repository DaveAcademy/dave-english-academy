Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Past Continuous: was/were + -ing for background action · interrupted by Past Simple
'was/were + -ing' — davom etgan harakat, o'tgan zamon — uni bo'lgan hodisa
I was reading when the phone rang.Kitob o'qiyotgan edim, qo'ng'iroq jiringladi.
While we were walking, it started to rain.Yurib ketayotgan edik, yomg'ir boshlandi.
He was sleeping at that moment.U o'sha paytda uxlayotgan edi.
❌
Wrong: *I was read a book.*
Right: *I was reading a book.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Missing Key

Last Friday, I was studying in my room when I heard my mother calling: 'Have you seen my keys?' I was helping her look when my little brother walked in. While he was eating a biscuit, he suddenly stopped. 'What's in my pocket?' he asked. We looked — it was the key! He was playing with it in the morning and forgot. In the end, everyone laughed, and my mother put the key on a hook. 'From now on,' she said, 'keys stay on this hook!'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What was the writer doing when mother called?
  2. What was the little brother doing?
  3. Where was the key?
  4. What did mother do in the end?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Story of the Storm

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What was the family doing when the storm started?
  2. What happened while they were watching?
  3. What did they do in the end?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Last week, while we were having dinner, a storm started. Suddenly, the lights went out. We were sitting in the dark when my father found candles. While he was lighting them, my little sister started to cry. 'Don't worry,' my mother said. 'This is an adventure!' In the end, we told stories by candlelight. It was actually fun!
