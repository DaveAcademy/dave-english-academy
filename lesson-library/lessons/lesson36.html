Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Imperatives: Turn left. / Turn right. / Go straight.
Buyruq gaplar: chapga buriling, o'ngga buriling, to'g'ri yuring
Turn left at the shop.Do'konda chapga buriling.
Go straight and turn right.To'g'ri yuring va o'ngga buriling.
The park is on your left.Bog' sizning chapingizda.
❌
Wrong: *Go to left.*
Right: *Turn left.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. ___ left at the bank. (Turn/Go)
  2. Go ___ for two blocks. (straight/left)
  3. The school is on your ___. (left/under)
  4. Stop ___ the corner. (at/in)
Match the Pairs(Moslang)

Go straightTo'g'ri yuring
On your rightO'ngingizda

Circle the Answer(To'g'ri javobni belgilang)
  1. You are at the bank. The shop is two blocks ahead. What do you do? → a) Go straight b) Turn left c) Stop
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. left / Turn / the / at / shop / .
  2. your / park / on / is / The / right / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
READ
📖 A Trip to the Library

Ask for directions: 'Excuse me, where is the library?' 'Go straight ahead for two blocks. Turn left at the bank. The library is on your right, next to the park.' 'Thank you!' 'You're welcome!'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How many blocks do you go straight?
  2. Where do you turn left?
  3. Where is the library?

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Giving Directions — Lesson 36

  • Act out 'turn left / right / go straight' physically to make it memorable.
  • Draw a map on the board and practice with real locations.
  • Watch for 'go to left' — drill 'turn left'.
  • Role-play polite openers: 'Excuse me, ...'.
