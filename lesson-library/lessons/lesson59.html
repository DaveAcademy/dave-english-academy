Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Plan Your Trip
  1. Choose a place: mountains, lake, big city, village. / Joy tanlang.
  2. Choose transport: bus, car, train. / Transport tanlang.
  3. Write 4 activities: 'We're going to swim / take photos...' / 4 ta mashg'ulot yozing.
  4. Pack your backpack: list 5 items. / Ryukzakga nima olib borasiz?
  5. Present your plan to the class. / Rejani taqdim eting.
Match the Pairs(Moslang)

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
Present Your Trip

Present: where, how you're going, 4 activities. / Qayerga, qanday, nima qilasiz — taqdim eting.

Compare and Choose

Compare two trip ideas: 'The mountains are cooler than the city.' Which is better? / Ikki rejani taqqoslang.

Vote(Real-life Mission)

Class votes for the best trip plan! / Sinf eng yaxshi rejaga ovoz bersin.

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Fill: 'We're going to ___ camping.' (go/going)
  2. Fill: 'I'm going to take ___ .' (photos/photoing)
  3. Translate: 'Tog'lar go'zal.' → __
  4. Name 3 trip items in English.
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Write your final trip plan (6 sentences with going to) and draw a small map. / Sayohat rejangizni yozing.
