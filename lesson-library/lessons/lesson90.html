Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Describe customs: In + country, people + verb · It is a tradition to + verb
'In + mamlakat, people + fe'l' — urf-odatlar
In Japan, people bow when they meet.Yaponiyada uchrashganda ta'zim qilinadi.
In Italy, people celebrate with big family dinners.Italiyada katta oilaviy kechki ovqatlar bilan nishonlanadi.
It is a tradition to drink tea in Uzbekistan.O'zbekistonda choy ichish an'ana.
❌
Wrong: *In Japan people is bowing.*
Right: *In Japan people bow.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Festival in My School

Our school held an international festival. Each class chose a country. My class chose Japan. We learned that in Japan, people bow when they greet, and they take off their shoes at home. We decorated our corner with paper flowers and taught everyone to write 'hello' in Japanese. The Korean class showed a traditional dance. The Turkish class served tea in small glasses. At the end, our teacher said: 'Look at us — different clothes, different food, but one happy school. That is what a global world should be.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What did the writer's class choose?
  2. What did they learn about Japan?
  3. What did the Korean class show?
  4. What did the teacher say at the end?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Customs Around the World

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What do people do in Thailand?
  2. What do people in Brazil love?
  3. What did the speaker learn?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Today I learned about customs. In Thailand, people press their hands together to say hello. In Brazil, people love festivals and dancing — especially in February. In some places, it is rude to refuse food. Every custom is different, but all of them show respect. Learning about them makes the world friendlier.
