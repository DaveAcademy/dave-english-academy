Grammar Time!

Grammatika
There is a park near my house. / The school is far from my house.
'near' — yaqin, 'far from' — ...dan uzoq
There is a park near my house.Uyim yonida bog' bor.
The hospital is far from the center.Kasalxona markazdan uzoqda.
There are many shops on this street.Bu ko'chada ko'p do'konlar bor.
❌
Wrong: *The shop is near to my house.*
Right: *The shop is near my house.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. There is a park ___ my house. (near/near to)
  2. The bank is far ___ the school. (from/at)
  3. There ___ many shops on this street. (is/are)
  4. ___ there a hospital near here? (Is/Are)
Match the Pairs(Moslang)
EnglishUzbek
ParkBog'
ShopDo'kon
HospitalKasalxona
PlaygroundO'yin maydonchasi
Circle the Answer(To'g'ri javobni belgilang)
  1. Is there a bank near here? → a) Yes, there is b) Yes, there are c) Yes, it is
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. a / near / There / is / park / my / house / .
  2. far / is / hospital / The / from / here / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
READ
📖 My Street

My street is nice. There is a small shop next to my house. The school is near the shop. Behind the school there is a big playground. Children play there every day. The park is far from my street, but my family goes there on Sundays. We love our neighborhood!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What is next to my house?
  2. Where is the playground?
  3. When does the family go to the park?

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Translate: 'Bog' uyimga yaqin.' → __
  2. The hospital is far ___ the school. (from/at)
  3. Fill in: 'There ___ many shops on this street.' (is/are)
  4. Name 3 places in a neighborhood.
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Vocabulary: 10 words 3× · Grammar: 5 sentences about places near your home (near/far from) · Reading: re-read 'My Street', answer 3 questions · Speaking: tell your family about your street.

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 My Neighborhood — Lesson 32

  • Use a simple map on the board and drill near/far from with real positions.
  • Review 'there is/are' before the new 'near/far' phrases.
  • Watch for 'near to' — correct to 'near'.
  • For homework, students can draw and label a map of their street in English.
