Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Formal application: Dear... → I am writing to apply for → my skills include → I am available → Yours sincerely
Rasmiy ariza tuzilishi
I am writing to apply for the position of assistant.Yordamchi lavozimiga ariza yozyapman.
My skills include English, computers and teamwork.Mahoratlarim: ingliz tili, kompyuter, jamoada ishlash.
I am available for an interview any day.Suhbatga istalgan kun tayyorman.
❌
Wrong: *I want the job. Give it to me.* (too rude)
Right: *I would be happy to work with you.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Successful Application

When Zarina saw the job advertisement, she smiled. She had the right skills. She wrote a formal application: 'Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to apply for the position of shop assistant. I have one year of experience in a bookshop. My skills include customer service and computers. I am hard-working and responsible. I am available for an interview any day. Yours sincerely, Zarina.' The manager read it and thought: 'Clear, polite, confident.' He called her the next day.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What job did Zarina apply for?
  2. What experience does she have?
  3. What does she say about availability?
  4. What did the manager think of the letter?

🎧 The Interview Call

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Manager: 'Hello, is this Zarina?' Zarina: 'Yes, speaking.' Manager: 'This is Mr. Karimov. We liked your application. Can you come for an interview on Friday at 10?' Zarina: 'Yes, of course!' Manager: 'Please bring your qualification papers and a reference.' Zarina: 'I will. Thank you very much!'
