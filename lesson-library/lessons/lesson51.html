Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Short adjectives: -er + than (big→bigger, fast→faster) · long: more + adj + than (more expensive)
Qisqa sifatlar -er, uzunlari 'more' oladi
A bike is faster than a car? No — a car is faster than a bike.Mashina velosipeddan tez.
This phone is more expensive than that one.Bu telefon anavadan qimmat.
Summer is hotter than winter.Yoz qishdan issiq.
❌
Wrong: *more big*
Right: *bigger*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Two Cities

Tashkent and Samarkand are two wonderful cities. Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand and it has more people. Samarkand is older — it is more than 2,700 years old! Tashkent is busier, with more cars and shops. Samarkand is quieter and more beautiful, with its blue domes. Food is cheaper in Samarkand. Both cities are beautiful in their own way.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Which city is bigger?
  2. Which city is older?
  3. Where is food cheaper?
  4. Which city is quieter?
