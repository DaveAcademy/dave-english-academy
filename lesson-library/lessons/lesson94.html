Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My Story

Tell a true story (2 minutes): start, what happened, the surprise, the end. / Haqiqiy hikoyangizni ayting.

Listener Questions

Your partner asks 2 questions about your story. Answer them. / Juftingiz savol bersin.

The Best Story(Real-life Mission)

The class votes for the most interesting and funniest story. / Eng yaxshi hikoya.

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Translate: 'To'satdan yomg'ir boshlandi.' → __
  2. Translate: 'Oxir-oqibat hamma kuldi.' → __
  3. Fill: 'I was ___ when it happened.' (walking/walk)
  4. What makes a story interesting?
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Write your best story (10-12 sentences) and practice telling it without reading. / Hikoyangizni yozing va aytib mashq qiling.
