Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Agree: I agree / Exactly / That's true · Disagree: I'm afraid I disagree / I see your point, but...
Rozilik va rozi bo'lmaslik iboralari
I completely agree with you.Siz bilan to'liq roziman.
I'm afraid I disagree — prices are going up.Afsuski, rozimasman — narxlar oshmoqda.
I see your point, but I think it's too expensive.Fikringizni tushunaman, lekin bu qimmat.
❌
Wrong: *You are wrong!* (too rude)
Right: *I'm afraid I disagree.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A School Discussion

Our class discussed school uniforms. 'I completely agree with uniforms,' said Madina. 'They make everyone equal.' 'That's true,' said Timur. 'However, I think students should choose their own style.' 'In fact, many countries don't have uniforms,' added Kamola. 'Perhaps we should try a year without them.' The teacher nodded. 'You all have good points. A respectful discussion is the best way to learn.' Everyone left with something to think about.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Why does Madina like uniforms?
  2. What does Timur say however?
  3. What does Kamola say about other countries?
  4. What did the teacher say about the discussion?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Debate

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What does the first speaker agree with?
  2. What does the second speaker disagree with?
  3. How do they end the discussion?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Speaker 1: 'In my opinion, animals should not live in zoos.' Speaker 2: 'I see your point, but zoos protect animals from danger.' Speaker 1: 'Perhaps, but cages are too small.' Speaker 2: 'That's true. However, many zoos are improving.' Speaker 1: 'Let's agree to respect both views.' Speaker 2: 'Agreed!'
