Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build a Weekend Plan
  1. Choose a day: Saturday or Sunday. / Kun tanlang.
  2. Choose 3 activities: picnic, museum, cinema, park, market... / 3 ta mashg'ulot tanlang.
  3. Write times: 'At 10 we're going to...' / Vaqtlarni yozing.
  4. Invite a partner: 'Would you like to join us?' / Juftingizni taklif qiling.
  5. Present your plan to the class. / Rejani taqdim eting.

Speak Up!

Invite and Accept

Invite your partner to your plan. They accept or decline politely. / Juftingizni taklif qiling — u qabul qilsin yoki rad etsin.

Vote(Real-life Mission)

❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Fill: 'We're going to ___ a picnic.' (have/having)
