Grammar Time!

Grammatika
I have a cold/a headache/a fever · My ... hurts · You should...
'I have a...' — kasallik nomi; 'should' — maslahat
I have a cold and a sore throat.Shamollab, tomog'im og'riyapti.
My stomach hurts.Qornim og'riyapti.
You should take this medicine and rest.Bu dorini iching va dam oling.
❌
Wrong: *I have headache.*
Right: *I have a headache.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Lola's Sick Day

Lola woke up with a sore throat and a high temperature. 'I have a headache too,' she told her mother. Mother checked her temperature: 38.5 degrees. 'You have a fever. You should stay in bed today,' she said. Lola drank warm tea, took her medicine and slept. By evening, she felt a little better. 'Rest is the best medicine,' her mother smiled.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What did Lola wake up with?
  2. What was her temperature?
  3. What did the mother say?
  4. How did Lola feel by evening?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Phone Call to the Doctor

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What is wrong with the boy?
  2. What does the doctor advise?
  3. When should he call again?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Boy: 'My stomach hurts and I feel tired.' Doctor: 'Did you eat something bad?' Boy: 'Maybe.' Doctor: 'Drink warm water and rest. Don't eat heavy food today. If it gets worse, call me tomorrow.'
