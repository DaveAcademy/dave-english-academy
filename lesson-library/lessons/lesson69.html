Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Advice: should + verb (recommendation) · shouldn't + verb (warning)
'Should' — maslahat, 'shouldn't' — ogohlantirish
You should drink more water.Ko'proq suv ichishingiz kerak.
You shouldn't eat too much sugar.Ko'p shakar yemasligingiz kerak.
Children should sleep 8-10 hours.Bolalar 8-10 soat uxlashi kerak.
❌
Wrong: *You should to eat fruit.*
Right: *You should eat fruit.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Healthier Me

Last month, Sardor felt tired every day. His doctor said: 'You should sleep more and eat better.' So Sardor made a plan. He started to eat fresh fruit and vegetables. He drank more water and less cola. He exercised for thirty minutes every day. Now Sardor has more energy. 'I feel great!' he says. 'Small healthy habits changed my life.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Why did Sardor see a doctor?
  2. What did the doctor say?
  3. What did Sardor change?
  4. How does he feel now?
