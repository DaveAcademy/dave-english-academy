Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My Strong Opinion

Speak for 2 minutes on one topic: opinion, 2 reasons, 1 example. / Kuchli fikringiz haqida gapiring.

The Panel

Form a panel of 3. Present opinions, ask questions, vote on the best view. / Uch kishilik panel tuzing.

Active Listening(Real-life Mission)

Summarize one opinion you heard in 2 sentences. / Bitta fikrni xulosalang.

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Translate: 'Fikrimcha, bu yaxshi g'oya.' → __
  2. Translate: 'Afsuski, rozimasman.' → __
  3. Fill: 'I ___ with you.' (agree/am agree)
  4. Give one reason for your opinion.
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Write your opinion on one topic (8-10 sentences) with reasons. Practice speaking it. / Fikringizni yozing.
