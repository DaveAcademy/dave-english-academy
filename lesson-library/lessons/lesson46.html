Grammar Time!

Grammatika
What does he/she do? — He/She is a... (+ what they do at work)
'U nima ish qiladi?' — 'U...'
What does your father do? — He's a driver.Otangiz nima ish qiladi? — U haydovchi.
What does she do? — She's a nurse.U nima ish qiladi? — U hamshira.
He flies planes.U samolyot boshqaradi.
❌
Wrong: *What your father does?*
Right: *What does your father do?*

Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. What ___ your mother do? (does/do)
  2. She ___ a teacher. (is/does)
  3. He ___ planes. (flies/fly)
  4. My father ___ a doctor. (is/does)

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 People in My Family

Let me tell you about my family. My father, Akmal, is a farmer. He grows wheat and vegetables. My mother, Dilnoza, is a teacher. She teaches English at a big school. My older brother is an engineer — he designs buildings. My aunt is a nurse at the city hospital. And me? I want to be a pilot when I grow up!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What does Akmal grow?
  2. Where does Dilnoza teach?
  3. What does the brother do?
  4. What does the writer want to be?
