Grammar Time!

Grammatika
How many + countable (apples, eggs) · How much + uncountable (milk, sugar)
Sanoqli bilan 'how many', sanoqsiz bilan 'how much'
How many eggs are there? — There are six.Nechta tuxum bor? — Oltita.
How much milk is there? — There's a little.Qancha sut bor? — Bir oz.
How many apples do you want?Nechta olma kerak?
❌
Wrong: *How much apples are there?*
Right: *How many apples are there?*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 At the Market

Nodir went to the market with his mother. 'How many carrots do we need?' his mother asked. 'Three kilos,' Nodir said. 'And how much rice?' asked the seller. 'Two kilos, please.' Nodir also wanted a little chocolate. His mother smiled: 'How much chocolate do you want?' 'A lot!' said Nodir, and everyone laughed.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How many kilos of carrots do they need?
  2. How much rice do they buy?
  3. What did Nodir want at the end?
