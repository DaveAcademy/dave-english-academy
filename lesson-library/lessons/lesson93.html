Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build Your Documentary
  1. Choose a topic: our school, a tradition, our town, a hobby. / Mavzu tanlang.
  2. Write the script: intro, 3 scenes, ending. / Ssenariy yozing.
  3. Choose roles: narrator, camera, actors, interviewer. / Rollar tanlang.
  4. Write interview questions (3) in English. / 3 ta savol yozing.
  5. Plan the scenes: what the camera shows. / Sahnani rejalang.
  6. Present your plan to the class. / Rejani taqdim eting.
