Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Sequence: First... Then... After that... Finally... + past tense
Tartib so'zlari + o'tgan zamon
First, we bought tickets.Avvalo bilet oldik.
Then, we waited for the train.Keyin poyezdni kutdik.
Finally, we arrived in Bukhara.Nihoyat Buxoroga yetib keldik.
❌
Wrong: *Then finally first we...*
Right: *First... then... finally.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Day in Khiva

Last week my class went on a trip to Khiva. First, we left school very early in the morning. The journey by bus took five hours. Then, we walked through the old city. The scenery was amazing — high blue towers and narrow streets. After that, we had lunch at a small café. Finally, we visited the museum and took many photos. It was a tiring but unforgettable day!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. When did the class leave?
  2. How long was the bus journey?
  3. What did they see in the old city?
  4. Where did they have lunch?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Mountain Trip

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Where did they go?
  2. What did they do first?
  3. What did they see on the mountain?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Last Sunday we went to the mountains. First, we drove for two hours. Then we walked up the mountain. The view from the top was amazing — green valleys and white clouds below us. After that, we had a picnic. Finally, we came down and drove home. What a day!
