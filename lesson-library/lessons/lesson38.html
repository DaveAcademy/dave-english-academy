Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My House and Town

Speak for 1 minute about your house and town. Use: There is/are, near/far, turn left/right. / 1 daqiqa gapiring.

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Describe your room in 2 sentences.
  2. What does 'go straight' mean in Uzbek?
  3. Name 3 places in a neighborhood.
  4. Complete: 'The bank is ___ the corner.' (on/at)
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Write a short paragraph about your town (5-6 sentences). Practice speaking it aloud. / Shahringiz haqida qisqa matn yozing.
