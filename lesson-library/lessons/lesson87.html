Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
The Debate Plan
  1. Choose a topic: 'Social media is good for students' or 'Homework should be smaller'. / Mavzu tanlang.
  2. Split into two teams: For and Against. / Ikkiga bo'ling.
  3. Each team writes 3 reasons with examples. / 3 ta dalil yozing.
  4. Team 1 speaks (1 minute), Team 2 listens. / Jamoa 1 gapiradi.
  5. Team 2 responds politely. Then Team 1 responds. / Javob bering.
  6. Judges decide the winner. / Hakamlar hal qiladi.

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
Your Argument

Present your argument: opinion + 2 reasons + 1 conditional sentence. / Fikringizni asoslang.

The Cross-Examination

Ask your opponent one question. Answer theirs politely. / Raqibingizga savol bering.

The Vote(Real-life Mission)

Judges give feedback and the class votes. / Hakamlar fikr beradi.
