Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Free Time Discussion Cards
  1. What is your favourite hobby? / Sevimli mashg'ulotingiz?
  2. How often do you do it? / Qanchalik tez-tez qilasiz?
  3. What do you do on Sundays? / Yakshanba kuni nima qilasiz?
  4. Do you like watching movies? What kind? / Kino ko'rishni yoqtirasizmi?
  5. Is there any hobby you want to try? / Qaysi mashg'ulotni sinab ko'rmoqchisiz?

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My Free Time

Speak for 2 minutes about your free time: your favourite hobby, how often you do it, what you do at weekends. / Bo'sh vaqtingiz haqida 2 daqiqa gapiring.

Quiz & Homework

  2. Translate: 'Ba'zan rasm chizaman.' → __
