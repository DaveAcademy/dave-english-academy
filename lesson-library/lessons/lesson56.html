Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Weekend story: regular + irregular past verbs in time order
Dam olish hikoyasi: o'tgan zamon fe'llari tartibda
On Saturday I went fishing.Shanba kuni baliq oviga bordim.
In the evening we had a barbecue.Kechqurun barbekyu qildik.
On Sunday I rested and watched a film.Yakshanba kuni dam oldim va film ko'rdim.
❌
Wrong: *On Sunday I watch a film.*
Right: *On Sunday I watched a film.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Perfect Weekend

Last weekend was perfect. On Saturday morning, my family and I went to the mountains. We walked for two hours and saw beautiful views. My father took many photos. In the evening we came home and had a barbecue in the garden. On Sunday, we visited my grandparents. My grandmother cooked my favourite food. I had a wonderful weekend!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where did the family go on Saturday?
  2. How long did they walk?
  3. What did they do in the evening?
  4. Who did they visit on Sunday?
