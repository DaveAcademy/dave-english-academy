Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build the Clinic Scene
  1. Choose roles: patient, receptionist, nurse, doctor, pharmacist. / Rollar tanlang.
  2. Patient: say your symptoms. / Bemor alomatlarini aytadi.
  3. Receptionist: take the card, tell the patient to wait. / Registrator kartani oladi.
  4. Nurse: check temperature. / Hamshira haroratni o'lchaydi.
  5. Doctor: examine, give advice and a prescription. / Shifokor maslahat beradi.
  6. Pharmacist: give the medicine with instructions. / Dorixonachi dori beradi.
  7. Swap roles and repeat! / Rollarni almashtiring!
