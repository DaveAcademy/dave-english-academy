Grammar Time!

Grammatika
have to + verb (necessary) · don't/doesn't have to + verb (not necessary)
'have to' — majburiyat; 'don't have to' — shart emas
I have to wear a uniform.Forma kiyishim kerak.
She doesn't have to work on Friday.Juma kuni ishlashi shart emas.
We have to finish the report today.Hisobotni bugun tugatishimiz kerak.
❌
Wrong: *She has to works.*
Right: *She has to work.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Nurse's Day

Malika is a nurse at a city hospital. She has to wake up at five o'clock and be at work by seven. She has to wear a clean uniform and check the patients in her ward. Some days she works overtime when there are many patients. She doesn't have to work on Saturdays — that is her rest day. Malika loves her job. 'I have to be kind and careful every day,' she says. 'My colleagues help me, and my boss is fair.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What time does Malika have to wake up?
  2. What does she have to wear?
  3. When doesn't she have to work?
  4. What does Malika love about her job?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The New Job

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What time does the man have to start?
  2. What does he have to wear?
  3. When doesn't he have to work?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. 'So, you are the new employee. You have to start at 8 o'clock and wear a uniform. You don't have to work on Sundays. On Fridays we have a short meeting.' 'Do I have to work overtime?' 'Only in busy weeks.' 'OK, I understand. Thank you!'
