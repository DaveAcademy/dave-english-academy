Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Plan the Trip
  1. Choose a place and say why it is interesting. / Joy tanlang va sababini ayting.
  2. Choose transport and estimate the cost. / Transport va narxni hisoblang.
  3. Make a schedule: 'First we... Then we... Finally we...' / Dastur tuzing.
  4. Check the budget: entrance fees, food, transport. / Byudjetni tekshiring.
  5. Present your plan — the class votes! / Rejani taqdim eting.

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
Present Your Plan

Present your trip: place, transport, schedule, budget. (5-7 sentences) / Sayohat rejangizni taqdim eting.

Persuade Me

Convince your partner your trip is the best: 'It's more interesting than... / cheaper than...'. / Rejangiz eng yaxshiligini isbotlang.

Class Vote(Real-life Mission)

The class votes for one trip. The winner becomes the real plan! / Sinf ovoz bersin.
