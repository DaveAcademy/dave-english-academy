Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build Your Map
  1. Draw a map of a small town with 6 places (bank, shop, school, park, hospital, your house). / 6 ta joyli shaharcha xaritasi chizing.
  2. Label every place in English. / Har bir joyni inglizcha yozing.
  3. Write directions from your house to the school. / Uyingizdan maktabgacha yo'l yozing.
  4. Check your partner's directions. / Juftingiz yo'lini tekshiring.

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
Guide Your Partner

Your partner's pen is a car. Give directions from one place to another on your map. / Juftingizga xaritada yo'l ko'rsating.

Tourist and Local(Real-life Mission)

Ask and answer: 'Excuse me, where is the...?' using your maps. / Xaritadan foydalanib so'rang va javob bering.

Quiz & Homework

Test va uy vazifasi
❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Give a direction: from the shop to the park (you draw the way).
  2. Translate: 'Xarita' → __
  3. Complete: 'Turn ___ at the bank.' (left/right)
  4. How do you ask politely? → 'Excuse me, ___ is the bank?'
🏠 Homework (Uyga vazifa)

Finish your map. Write 4 directions. Practice saying them aloud. / Xaritani tugating va yo'llarni mashq qiling.
