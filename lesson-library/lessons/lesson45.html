Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Polite requests: I'd like... / Can I have...? + quantities
Xushmuomala so'rov: 'I'd like' — men istayman
I'd like a kilo of rice, please.Iltimos, bir kilo guruch.
Can I have a bottle of milk?Bir shisha sut olsam bo'ladimi?
How much is that?Bu qancha turadi?
❌
Wrong: *I want a bread.*
Right: *I'd like some bread.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Quick Shopping Trip

Malika went to the supermarket with a short list. First she took a trolley. In the fruit aisle she put two kilos of apples. In aisle four she found a packet of biscuits and a bottle of juice. At the checkout, she said: 'I'd like a bag, please.' The cashier smiled: 'That will be fifteen thousand som.' Malika paid and took her receipt. She was home in ten minutes.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where did Malika put the apples?
  2. What did she buy in aisle four?
  3. What did she ask for at the checkout?
  4. How much did she pay?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 At the Checkout

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What does the customer buy?
  2. How much is it?
  3. What does the customer ask for at the end?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Customer: 'I'd like a kilo of cheese, please.' Cashier: 'Here you are.' Customer: 'How much is that?' Cashier: 'Thirty thousand som.' Customer: 'Can I have a bag, please?' Cashier: 'Of course.'
