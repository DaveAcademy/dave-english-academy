Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Comparative: -er / more + than · Superlative: the -est / the most
Taqqoslash va ustun daraja
Tashkent is bigger than Fergana.Toshkent Farg'onadan katta.
Bukhara is older than Tashkent.Buxoro Toshkentdan qadimiy.
Tashkent is the biggest city in Uzbekistan.Toshkent O'zbekistondagi eng katta shahar.
❌
Wrong: *the more biggest*
Right: *the biggest*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Two Lives

Aziza lives in Tashkent, and her cousin Bek lives in a village near Andijan. Tashkent is bigger and busier than the village. It has more schools, shops and hospitals, but the traffic is heavy. In the village, life is quieter. Bek says the air is cleaner and people are friendlier. 'The village is cheaper and calmer,' he says. 'But I love visiting Tashkent for the cinemas and parks.' Both places are special — in different ways.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where does Bek live?
  2. What does Tashkent have more of?
  3. What does Bek like about the village?
  4. What does Aziza's cousin like about Tashkent?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 City or Village?

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Why does the woman prefer the village?
  2. Why does the man prefer the city?
  3. Where do they both agree food is cheaper?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Woman: 'I love the village. It's quieter and the air is cleaner.' Man: 'But the city has more jobs and better schools.' Woman: 'True, but food is cheaper in the village.' Man: 'And transport is better in the city. Every place has good and bad points!'
