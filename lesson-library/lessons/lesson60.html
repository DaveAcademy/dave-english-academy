Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Review Stations
  1. Past: 'Yesterday I went to...' — tell 3 things. / Kecha qilgan 3 ishingizni ayting.
  2. Going to: 'Next weekend I'm going to...' — 3 plans. / Keyingi rejalaringiz.
  3. Compare: '... is better than ...' — 2 comparisons. / 2 taqqoslash.
  4. Frequency: 'I usually... / I never...' — 2 habits. / 2 odat.
  5. Invite: 'Would you like to...?' — invite your partner. / Taklif qiling.

Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My Story

Speak for 2 minutes mixing past and future: what you did last weekend and what you're going to do next. / O'tgan va kelajak haqida gapiring.
