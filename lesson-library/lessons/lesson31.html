Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Where is (singular) / Where are (plural) → It's / They're + place
Bitta narsa uchun 'where is', ko'pi uchun 'where are'
Where is the lamp? — It's on the desk.Chiroq qayerda? — U parta ustida.
Where are the shoes? — They're under the bed.Tuflilar qayerda? — Ular to'shak tagida.
Where is the cat? — It's behind the sofa.Mushuk qayerda? — U divan orqasida.
❌
Wrong: *Where the pen is?*
Right: *Where is the pen?*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. Where ___ the mirror? (is/are)
  2. Where ___ the chairs? (is/are)
  3. The cat is ___ the sofa. (under/on)
  4. The lamp is ___ the table. (on/in)
Match the Pairs(Moslang)
EnglishUzbek
Where is the lamp?It's on the desk.
Where are the chairs?They're next to the table.
Where is the bed?It's in the bedroom.
Circle the Answer(To'g'ri javobni belgilang)
  1. Where is the mirror? → a) It's on the wall b) They're on the wall c) Yes, it is
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. is / the / Where / lamp / ?
  2. are / chairs / the / Where / ?
  3. the / is / cat / under / The / table / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
READ
📖 Ben's Room

Ben's room is small but nice. His bed is next to the window. His lamp is on the desk. His books are under the desk. There is a big wardrobe behind the door. His cat, Tom, is on the bed. Where are Ben's shoes? They're under the bed, of course!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where is Ben's lamp?
  2. Where are his books?
  3. Where is Tom the cat?
  4. Where are Ben's shoes?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Find It!

🎵 4 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Where is the ball first? (under the chair / on the table)
  2. Where is the ball second?
  3. Where is the ball third?
  4. Where is the ball last?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Let's play find it! Listen. The ball is under the chair. (pause) Now the ball is on the table. (pause) Now it is behind the door. (pause) Now it is next to the box.

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Where Is It? — Lesson 31

  • Play 'hide the object' to make position words physical.
  • Drill question word order: Where + is/are + noun.
  • Move a real ball as you read the listening script.
  • Watch for yes/no answers to where-questions; teach 'It's/They're + place'.
