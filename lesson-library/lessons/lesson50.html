Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Future plan: am/is/are going to + verb
'going to' + fe'l — kelajak reja
I am going to study English tonight.Bugun kechqurun inglizcha o'qiyman.
She is going to visit her aunt.U xolasinikiga boradi.
We are going to have a party.Biz ziyofat qilamiz.
❌
Wrong: *I going to study.*
Right: *I am going to study.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Weekend Plans

Ask the family: what are your plans? 'Tonight we're going to watch a film together,' says Mum. 'On Saturday, Dad and I are going to visit the bazaar,' says Grandmother. 'I'm going to play football with my friends,' says brother. 'And I'm going to finish my book!' says sister. Everyone has a plan — and I am going to help Mum cook dinner!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What is the family going to do tonight?
  2. What are the grandmother and Dad going to do on Saturday?
  3. What is the brother going to do?
  4. What is the writer going to do?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 What's the Plan?

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What is Anna going to do tomorrow?
  2. Where is she going to travel?
  3. Who is she going to meet?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Anna has big plans. Tomorrow she is going to get up early. She is going to travel to Bukhara with her family. There, she is going to meet her cousins and visit the old city.
