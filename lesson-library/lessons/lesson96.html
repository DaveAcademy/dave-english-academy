Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Email structure: greeting → purpose → details → closing · formal vs informal tone
Email: salom → maqsad → tafsilot → xulosa
Formal: Dear Mr. Karimov, I am writing to inform you...Rasmiy: 'Hurmatli janob Karimov...'
Informal: Hi Aziz! Just writing to say...Norasmiy: 'Salom Aziz!'
Best regards / Yours sincerely'Hurmat bilan'
❌
Wrong: email with no subject line
Right: *always add a subject*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Teacher's Email

Last week, our teacher sent an email to the class. The subject was 'Class trip — important!'. It said: 'Dear students, I am writing to inform you that the trip to Khiva is on Saturday. Please arrive at school at 7 a.m. Bring water and comfortable shoes. Reply to this email if you are coming. Best regards, Mr. Rahimov.' My friend replied: 'Hi Mr. Rahimov, I will come. Thanks!' We all smiled at his informal email — but the teacher didn't mind.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What was the subject of the email?
  2. What time must students arrive?
  3. What should they bring?
  4. What did the friend's reply say?

🎧 The Email to the Manager

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Why is the girl writing?
  2. What does she attach?
  3. What does she ask?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Girl: 'I need to email the manager. I'm writing to ask about the school project.' Friend: 'Attach your questions.' Girl: 'Good idea. Subject: Project questions. Dear Mrs. Azizova, I am writing to ask about the history project. When is the deadline? Please find my questions in the attachment. Thank you. Best regards, Nigora.' Friend: 'Perfect — clear and polite.'
