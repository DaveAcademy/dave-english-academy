Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Advice and rules: should/shouldn't + verb · never + verb (imperative)
Online maslahat: 'should/shouldn't'
You shouldn't share your password.Parolingizni bo'lishmasligingiz kerak.
Never post your home address.Uy manzilingizni hech qachon joylamang.
You should keep your profile private.Profilni maxfiy tutishingiz kerak.
❌
Wrong: *You should to keep.*
Right: *You should keep.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Safe Account

Zarina loves social media. She posts photos of her paintings and reads the comments happily. But she is careful. Her account is private, so only her friends can see it. She never shares her password, not even with her best friend. She never posts her address or her school name. Last week, a stranger sent her a message. Zarina didn't reply — she blocked him and told her parents. 'Social media is fun,' she says, 'if you are smart.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What does Zarina post?
  2. Why is her account private?
  3. What did the stranger send?
  4. What did Zarina do?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Two Opinions

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What does the girl like about social media?
  2. What does the boy think?
  3. What rule do they agree on?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Girl: 'I love social media! I see my friends' photos and learn new things.' Boy: 'Sometimes it's too much. People spend hours looking at screens.' Girl: 'That's true. But it's useful for school projects.' Boy: 'Let's agree: use it, but not too much.' Girl: 'Deal!'
