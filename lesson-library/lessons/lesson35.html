Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Describing: There is/are + noun + preposition + place
Xonani tasvirlash tartibi
There are white curtains on the window.Derazada oq pardalar bor.
There is a mirror between the pictures.Oyna rasmlar orasida.
The carpet is under the table.Gilam stol tagida.
❌
Wrong: *There is two pictures.*
Right: *There are two pictures.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. There ___ blue curtains on the window. (is/are)
  2. There is a clock ___ the wall. (on/in)
  3. The mirror is ___ the two pictures. (between/under)
  4. There ___ a vase on the shelf. (is/are)
Match the Pairs(Moslang)
EnglishUzbek
CurtainsParda
ShelfTokcha
CarpetGilam

Circle the Answer(To'g'ri javobni belgilang)
  1. Where is the mirror? → a) It's on the wall b) It's under the floor c) It's in the shelf
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. There / on / clock / is / wall / a / the / .
  2. curtains / are / on / white / the / window / There / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
READ
📖 Anna's Living Room

Anna's living room is warm and nice. There are red curtains on the window. There is a big sofa in front of the window. The carpet on the floor is soft. On the wall there are three pictures and a clock. There is a small table between the sofa and the door. On the table there is a vase with flowers. Anna loves her living room!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What color are the curtains?
  2. Where is the sofa?
  3. What is on the wall?
  4. What is on the table?

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Describing a Room — Lesson 35

  • Bring or draw a colorful room picture to make the vocabulary concrete.
  • Model a full description, then students copy the pattern for their own room.
  • Watch for missing 'there is/are' agreement.
  • Encourage adjectives (color, size) to enrich descriptions.
