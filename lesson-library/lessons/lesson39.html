Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Numbers: hundred / thousand · Prices: How much is/are...? — It's / They're ...
Sonlar: yuz, ming. Narx so'rash: qancha turadi?
One hundred and twenty som.Yuz yigirma som.
How much is the book? — It's five thousand som.Kitob qancha turadi? — Besh ming som.
How much are the shoes? — They're cheap!Tuflilar qancha? — Ular arzon!
❌
Wrong: *How much it is?*
Right: *How much is it?*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
READ
📖 The Market

Today is market day! I have two thousand som. First, I buy bread — it is three hundred som. Then I buy apples — they are five hundred som. I see a nice blue bag, but it is very expensive — four thousand som! I ask: 'Is the bag cheaper?' The seller says: 'Two thousand five hundred.' I pay and get my change. Now I have no money, but I am happy!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How much is the bread?
  2. How much are the apples?
  3. Why doesn't the boy buy the blue bag at first?
