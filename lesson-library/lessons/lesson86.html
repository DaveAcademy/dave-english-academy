Grammar Time!

Grammatika
First conditional: If + present, will + verb (real future possibility)
'If + hozirgi zamon, will + fe'l'
If it rains, I will stay home.Yomg'ir yog'sa, uyda qolaman.
If you study, you will pass the test.O'qisangiz, testdan o'tasiz.
If we hurry, we will catch the bus.Shoshilsak, avtobusga yetamiz.
❌
Wrong: *If it will rain, I stay.*
Right: *If it rains, I will stay.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Weather Test

The school trip to the mountains was on Saturday. But the weather was uncertain. 'If it rains, we will cancel the trip,' said the teacher. 'If it is cloudy, we will still go, but we'll take umbrellas. If it is sunny, we'll have a picnic!' The students waited nervously. On Friday night, the forecast said: sunny! 'If we go, I'll take my camera,' said Anvar. 'If my camera works, I'll take a photo of everyone!' Everyone laughed. The trip was on.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What will happen if it rains?
  2. What will they do if it is cloudy?
  3. What will Anvar do if they go?
  4. What did the forecast say?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 If I Have Time

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What will the girl do if she has time?
  2. What will she do if her friend comes?
  3. What will happen if the shop is closed?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Girl: 'If I have time after school, I'll go to the bookshop.' Friend: 'If I come with you, will you help me choose a book?' Girl: 'Of course! If the bookshop is closed, we'll go to the library instead.' Friend: 'Great. If we meet at 4, we'll have plenty of time.'
