Grammar Time!

Grammatika
must + verb (obligation) · mustn't + verb (forbidden) · imperatives for safety
'must' — shart, 'mustn't' — taqiq
You must call an ambulance.Tez yordam chaqirishingiz kerak.
You mustn't touch a burning wire.Yonayotgan simga tegish mumkin emas.
Stay calm and call 103.Sokin bo'ling va 103 ga qo'ng'iroq qiling.
❌
Wrong: *You must to call.*
Right: *You must call.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Quick Decision

On Saturday, Umida saw smoke coming from her neighbour's kitchen window. She called 101 immediately and told the operator the address. 'You mustn't go inside,' the operator warned. Umida stayed outside and guided her neighbours to a safe place. When the firefighters arrived, the fire was still small because of her quick call. 'You were brave and calm,' the chief said. Umida smiled. She knew the rules.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What did Umida see?
  2. What number did she call?
  3. What did the operator warn?
  4. Why did the fire stay small?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Emergency Call

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What is the emergency?
  2. What is the address?
  3. What must the caller do until help arrives?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Operator: 'Emergency, which service?' Caller: 'There's a car accident on Mustaqillik street!' Operator: 'Is anyone hurt?' Caller: 'Yes, a man is hurt.' Operator: 'Stay calm. Don't move him. An ambulance is coming now.'
