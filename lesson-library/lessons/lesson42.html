Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Irregular pasts: go→went, have→had, do→did, see→saw, eat→ate, buy→bought
Noto'g'ri fe'llar — shakllarini yodlang
I went to the market yesterday.Kecha bozorga bordim.
She saw her friend at school.U maktabda do'stini ko'rdi.
They bought a new phone.Ular yangi telefon sotib oldi.
❌
Wrong: *I go to the park yesterday.*
Right: *I went to the park yesterday.*

  2. She ___ (see) a film last night.
  3. We ___ (have) a big dinner.
  4. He ___ (buy) a new shirt.

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Weekend Visit

Last Saturday, Aziza and her brother went to their uncle's farm. They saw many animals — cows, sheep and a white horse. They ate fresh bread and drank cold milk. Aziza took some photos. Her brother helped the farmer. In the evening they came home tired and happy. Aziza said: 'What a great day!'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where did Aziza go on Saturday?
  2. What animals did they see?
  3. What did Aziza's brother do?
  4. What did Aziza say at the end?

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  2. What did Ali eat for breakfast?
  3. Where did Ali go after school?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Yesterday Ali got up at half past six. He had bread and milk for breakfast. After school, he went to his friend's house. They played games and made pizza. Ali came home at seven o'clock.

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Past Simple: Irregular Verbs — Lesson 42

  • Play memory games with the irregular pairs (matching cards).
  • Teach irregulars in groups by sound: go/went, see/saw, eat/ate.
  • Watch for 'goed'/'buyed' — correct to went/bought.
  • Encourage storytelling; students love sharing real weekend stories.
