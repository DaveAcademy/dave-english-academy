Grammar Time!

Grammatika
am/is/are + verb-ing (do → doing, run → running, write → writing)
'am/is/are' + fe'lning -ing shakli
I am reading a book now.Hozir kitob o'qiyapman.
She is cooking dinner.U kechki ovqat pishirmoqda.
They are playing football.Ular futbol o'ynamoqda.
❌
Wrong: *She is cook dinner.*
Right: *She is cooking dinner.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. I ___ (read) a book now.
  2. She ___ (cook) dinner.
  3. They ___ (play) in the park.
  4. Look! The dog ___ (run).

  1. What are you doing? → a) I'm writing b) I write c) I wrote
  2. She ___ now. → a) is sleeping b) sleep c) slept
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. is / cooking / She / now / .
  2. are / The / playing / children / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Busy Park

It is Sunday morning and the park is busy. Look! A man is running with his dog. Two girls are riding bikes. A boy is eating an ice cream. Under a tree, a woman is reading a book. Near the lake, some children are feeding the ducks. Everyone is having a wonderful time on this sunny morning.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What is the man doing?
  2. What are the two girls doing?
  3. Who is reading a book?
  4. What are the children doing near the lake?
