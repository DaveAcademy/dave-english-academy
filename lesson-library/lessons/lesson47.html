Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build Your Shop
  1. Make 5 items with price labels (in som). / 5 ta narxli mahsulot yasang.
  2. One of you is the customer, one is the cashier. / Biri xaridor, biri kassir bo'lsin.
  3. Use: 'I'd like... / Can I have...? / How much is that?' / Berilgan gaplarni ishlating.
  4. Swap roles and do it again. / Rollarni almashtiring.
