Grammar Time!

Grammatika
How do you celebrate...? / We usually... + traditions
'Qanday nishonlaysiz?' — odat va an'analar
How do you celebrate Nowruz?Navro'zni qanday nishonlaysiz?
We usually visit our grandparents and cook sumalak.Biz odatda buvijonnikiga boramiz va sumalak pishiramiz.
On this day, people decorate their houses.Bu kuni odamlar uylarini bezatadi.
❌
Wrong: *How you celebrate Nowruz?*
Right: *How do you celebrate Nowruz?*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Nowruz in Our Home

Nowruz is the most important holiday in our family. A week before, we clean the whole house and decorate the rooms with flowers. On the day, my grandmother cooks sumalak in a big pot — she starts at night and stirs it until morning! Our relatives come as guests, and we eat plov together. Children receive gifts and play outside. At night, we watch fireworks over the city. I love Nowruz because everyone is together.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What does the family do a week before Nowruz?
  2. What does grandmother cook?
  3. What do children receive?
  4. Why does the writer love Nowruz?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Eid Morning

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What time does the family get up?
  2. Where do they go in the morning?
  3. What do the children get?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. On Eid morning, we get up very early — at five o'clock. We put on our new clothes and go to the mosque. Then we visit our grandparents. We have a big breakfast with plov. The children get gifts and new clothes. In the afternoon, friends come to visit us. It is a happy day.
