Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Opinions: I think / In my opinion / I agree / I disagree + reason with because
Fikr: 'I think', sabab — 'because'
I think English is important because it helps you travel.Ingliz tili muhim, chunki sayohatda yordam beradi.
In my opinion, sport is fun.Fikrimcha, sport qiziqarli.
I agree with you. / I disagree — I think it's expensive.Roziman / Rozimasman.
❌
Wrong: *I am agree with you.*
Right: *I agree with you.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Books or Films?

Yesterday my class discussed a question: books or films? 'I think books are better,' said Gulnora. 'When you read, you imagine everything yourself. Films show you one person's picture.' 'In my opinion, films win,' said Jasur. 'They are quicker — two hours, and the story is over. Also, the music makes it exciting.' 'That's a good point,' said Gulnora, 'but a book stays with you for weeks.' The teacher smiled: 'The best opinion is to enjoy both.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Why does Gulnora prefer books?
  2. Why does Jasur prefer films?
  3. What did Gulnora say about books staying?
  4. What did the teacher say?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Opinions About City Life

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What does the first speaker think about city life?
  2. What does the second speaker disagree with?
  3. What do they agree on?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Speaker 1: 'In my opinion, city life is the best. There are cinemas, shops and many jobs.' Speaker 2: 'I disagree. Cities are too crowded and noisy. The village is calmer.' Speaker 1: 'But you must travel far for work.' Speaker 2: 'That's true. Both places have good points.'
