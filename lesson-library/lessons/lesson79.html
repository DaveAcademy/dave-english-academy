Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Build Your Interview
  1. Prepare answers: 'Tell me about yourself.' / O'zingiz haqida ayting.
  2. 'What are your skills?' — list 3. / 3 ta mahoratingiz.
  3. 'Why do you want this job?' — give a reason. / Sabab ayting.
  4. 'What are your strengths?' — name 2. / 2 ta kuchli tomon.
  5. Role-play: one employer, one candidate. Swap! / Rollarni almashtiring.
