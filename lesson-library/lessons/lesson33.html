Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Design Your Room
  1. Choose 5 pieces of furniture. / 5 ta mebel tanlang.
  2. Draw the room on paper. / Xonani qog'ozga chizing.
  3. Write 3 sentences with 'There is' and 2 with 'There are'. / 3 ta 'there is' va 2 ta 'there are' gap yozing.
  4. Name each thing in English. / Har bir narsani inglizcha ayting.

Speak Up!

Present Your Room

Show your drawing to the class. Say: 'This is my room. There is... There are...' / Xonangizni sinfga ko'rsating va tasvirlang.

Partner Questions(Real-life Mission)

Ask your partner 2 questions with 'Is there...?' about their room. / Juftingiz xonasi haqida 2 ta savol bering.

Quiz & Homework

❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Fill in: 'There ___ a mirror on the wall.' (is/are)
  2. Fill in: 'There ___ two sofas.' (is/are)
  3. Translate: 'Tokchada kitoblar bor.' → __
  4. Name 3 furniture words you learned today.
