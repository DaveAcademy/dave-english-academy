Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Invitations: Would you like to...? / Let's... / Do you want to...? · Accept: Yes, I'd love to · Decline: I'm sorry, I can't
Taklif: 'Would you like to...?', qabul: 'Yes, I'd love to'
Would you like to come to my party?Ziyofatimga kelasizmi?
Let's play football!Futbol o'ynaylik!
I'm sorry, I can't — I'm busy.Kechirasiz, kela olmayman — bandman.
❌
Wrong: *Would you like come?*
Right: *Would you like to come?*

Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. Would you like ___ come to my party? (to/for)
  2. Let's ___ chess. (play/playing)
  3. I'm sorry, I ___ come. (can't/don't)
  4. Are you ___ on Friday? (free/busy)

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Invitation

'Hi Aziz! Would you like to come to my birthday party on Saturday?' wrote Umid. Aziz smiled. 'Sure, I'd love to! What time?' 'At four o'clock,' Umid replied. 'But I'm sorry, my sister can't come — she is busy.' 'That's okay,' said Umid. 'Let's play football after the party!' Aziz agreed happily.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. When is the party?
  2. What time does it start?
  3. Why can't Umid's sister come?
  4. What will they do after the party?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Phone Call

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Who is calling?
  2. What does she invite him to?
  3. Can the boy come? Why?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. 'Hello, this is Sara. Would you like to come to my house tomorrow? We're going to make pizza!' 'Sure, I'd love to!' 'Great! And would you like to invite your brother too?' 'I'm sorry, he can't — he has football practice.'
