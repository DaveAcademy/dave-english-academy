Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Dreams: I want to be a... / I'm going to be a... / I'd like to... + reason with because
Orzu: 'I want to be' + sabab
I want to be a programmer because I love computers.Dasturchi bo'lmoqchiman, chunki kompyuterlarni yaxshi ko'raman.
She is going to be a doctor.U shifokor bo'ladi.
I'd like to help people.Odamlarga yordam berishni xohlayman.
❌
Wrong: *I want become a doctor.*
Right: *I want to become a doctor.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Three Dreams

Three friends, three dreams. Dilnoza wants to be a doctor because she likes helping sick people. She studies biology every day. Bobur is going to be a programmer — he loves computers and games, and wants to create his own game one day. Aziz dreams of being a pilot. He watches planes at the airport and reads about flying. Their teacher says: 'Dreams are important. Work hard, and they will come true.'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Why does Dilnoza want to be a doctor?
  2. What does Bobur want to create?
  3. What does Aziz dream of?
  4. What does the teacher say?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Career Interview

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What does the man do now?
  2. Why did he choose this job?
  3. What advice does he give?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Interviewer: 'What is your job?' Man: 'I'm an engineer. I design buildings.' Interviewer: 'Why did you choose this?' Man: 'Because I always loved maths and drawing. My advice: find what you love, then work hard. You will be successful.'
