Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Regular past: verb + -ed (play → played, watch → watched)
Muntazam fe'llarga '-ed' qo'shiladi
I played football yesterday.Kecha futbol o'ynadim.
She watched TV last night.U kecha televizor ko'rdi.
We cleaned the house last week.Biz o'tgan hafta uyni tozaladik.
❌
Wrong: *I play football yesterday.*
Right: *I played football yesterday.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Busy Day

Yesterday was a busy day for Tom. He started his day at seven o'clock. He studied English for one hour. Then he helped his father in the garden. In the afternoon he visited his grandmother. She cooked delicious soup. Tom watched a film in the evening and finished his homework. He was tired, but happy.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What time did Tom start his day?
  2. What did Tom do in the garden?
  3. What did his grandmother cook?
  4. How did Tom feel at the end of the day?

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Past Simple: Regular Verbs — Lesson 41

  • Review present routine verbs first, then add -ed.
  • Drill the -ed endings (/t/, /d/, /id/) — walked, cleaned, visited.
  • Watch for 'yesterday I play' — remind that yesterday needs past.
  • Writing task: model 2 sentences first, then students write.
