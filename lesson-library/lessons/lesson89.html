Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Discuss pros and cons: advantage/disadvantage + is · on the one hand / on the other hand
Afzallik/kamchilik: 'on the one hand... on the other hand'
On the one hand, cities have more jobs.Bir tomondan, shaharda ko'proq ish bor.
On the other hand, the countryside is peaceful.Boshqa tomondan, qishloq tinch.
An advantage of the village is fresh air.Qishloqning afzalligi — toza havo.
❌
Wrong: *The city has many advantagies.*
Right: *The city has many advantages.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Where Is Home?

My grandmother lives in a village, and I visit her every summer. On the one hand, the city has more facilities: big hospitals, modern schools and cinemas. On the other hand, the village gives me something the city cannot: fresh air, quiet nights and the smell of fresh bread. The city is convenient, but the countryside is peaceful. When I grow up, I want to work in the city but have a small house in the village. That would be perfect!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What facilities does the city have?
  2. What does the village give the writer?
  3. What does the writer want when he grows up?
  4. What does 'perfect' mean to the writer?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Decision

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Where does the woman want to live?
  2. What does the man prefer?
  3. What do they agree about?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Woman: 'I want to move to the village. Life is calmer there.' Man: 'But we need the city for work and school.' Woman: 'That's true. However, we could live on the edge of the city.' Man: 'Good point — we get nature and facilities!' Woman: 'Exactly! The best of both worlds.'
