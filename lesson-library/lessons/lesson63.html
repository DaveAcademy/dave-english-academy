Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Help phrases: Where is...? / Can I...? / How long does it take? / What time does it...?
Yordam so'rash iboralari
Where is the check-in desk?Ro'yxatdan o'tish qayerda?
Can I take this bag on the plane?Bu sumkani olib kirish mumkinmi?
What time does the flight depart?Reyis soat nechada jo'naydi?
❌
Wrong: *Where is check-in desk?*
Right: *Where is the check-in desk?*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 First Flight

It was Otabek's first flight. At the airport, he went to the check-in desk and showed his passport. The woman smiled and gave him a boarding pass. 'Your gate is number 7,' she said. Then Otabek went through security and waited near the gate. The flight was on time. When he sat by the window, he looked out — the city got smaller and smaller. It was wonderful!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where did Otabek go first at the airport?
  2. What did the woman give him?
  3. Which gate was his?
  4. Was the flight on time?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Airport Announcements

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Which flight is boarding?
  2. What is the gate number?
  3. Why is the other flight delayed?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen to the announcements. 'Attention, passengers. Flight 402 to Moscow is now boarding at gate 7.' 'Flight 305 to London is delayed by two hours because of the weather.' 'Passengers of flight 118, please go to gate 2.'
