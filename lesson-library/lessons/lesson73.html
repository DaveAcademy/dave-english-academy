Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Visit to the Doctor

Jasur felt sick, so his mother made an appointment. At the clinic, the receptionist took his card and told him to wait. Soon the doctor called him in. 'What's the problem?' the doctor asked. 'I have a sore throat and a headache,' said Jasur. The doctor examined him and checked his temperature. 'It's just a cold. Take this prescription to the pharmacy. Rest for two days and drink warm tea. You'll recover quickly.' Jasur felt better already.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Who made the appointment for Jasur?
  2. What did the receptionist do?
  3. What did the doctor examine?
  4. What did the doctor tell Jasur to do?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 At the Pharmacy

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. How often should the man take the tablets?
  2. How many tablets should he take?
  3. What else should he do?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Pharmacist: 'Here is your medicine. Take one tablet three times a day, after food.' Man: 'Anything else?' Pharmacist: 'Drink plenty of warm water and rest. Keep warm.' Man: 'Thank you!' Pharmacist: 'Get well soon!'
