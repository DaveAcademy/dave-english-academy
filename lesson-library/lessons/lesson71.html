Grammar Time!

Grammatika
My/his/her/our + body part · body part + hurts (it hurts)
Egalik + a'zo; 'hurts' — og'riyapti
My head hurts.Boshim og'riyapti.
His knee hurts after football.Futboldan keyin tizzasi og'riyapti.
She washed her hands.U qo'llarini yuvdi.
❌
Wrong: *My head hurt.*
Right: *My head hurts.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Football Injury

Yesterday Bek played football with his classmates. In the second half, he jumped for the ball and fell. 'Ow! My knee hurts!' he cried. His friend helped him up. At home, Bek put ice on his knee. His mother said: 'Rest today, and your leg will feel better tomorrow.' Bek washed his hands, ate dinner and rested. By morning, the knee felt much better.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What happened to Bek in the second half?
  2. What did he cry?
  3. How did Bek's mother help him?
  4. How did he feel by morning?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 At the Doctor's

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Which body part hurts?
  2. How did the accident happen?
  3. What does the doctor say?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Doctor: 'What's the problem?' Patient: 'My arm hurts.' Doctor: 'What happened?' Patient: 'I fell down the stairs.' Doctor: 'Let me look. Rest your arm for three days and put ice on it. It will be fine.'
