Speak Up!

Gapirish mashqi
My Yesterday

Speak for 2 minutes about your yesterday: morning, afternoon, evening. Use past verbs. / Kechagi kuningiz haqida 2 daqiqa gapiring.

Interview(Juftlikda)
AB
Did you get up early?Yes, I did. / No, I got up late.
Did you go out?Yes, I went out. / No, I stayed home.
Did you see any friends?Yes, I saw ...
Did you do your homework?Of course!
Active Listening(Real-life Mission)

Write 3 things your partner did yesterday. / Juftingizning 3 ta ishini yozing.

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Speaking Circle: Talking About Yesterday — Lesson 48

  • Speaking circle: keep each turn short and supportive.
  • Have a 'story chair' — one student narrates while others listen.
  • Correct past-tense errors gently by echoing the right form.
  • This consolidates L41-42 — no new grammar.
