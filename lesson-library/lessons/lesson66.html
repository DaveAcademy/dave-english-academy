Grammar Time!

Grammatika
It is a custom/tradition to + verb · describing what people usually do
'It is a tradition to...' — an'ana haqida
It is a custom to greet elders first.Kattalarga avval salom berish odat.
People usually share food with guests.Odamlar odatda mehmonlar bilan ovqat bo'lishadi.
Traditions are passed down from parents to children.An'analar ota-onadan bolalarga o'tadi.
❌
Wrong: *It is tradition to eat plov.*
Right: *It is a tradition to eat plov.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 An Uzbek Wedding

An Uzbek wedding is a joyful celebration full of traditions. First, the groom's family comes to the bride's house with gifts. Then everyone goes to the wedding hall. There is a ceremony called 'kelin salom' — the bride greets the guests and bows respectfully. People eat plov, dance and play music late into the night. The traditions are passed down from grandparents to children. Hospitality and respect are at the heart of every celebration.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What do the groom's family bring?
  2. What happens in the 'kelin salom' ceremony?
  3. What do people eat at the wedding?
  4. What is at the heart of Uzbek celebrations?

🎧 Traditions Around the World

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What happens in Japan?
  2. What do people do in Italy?
  3. Which tradition surprises the speaker?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Around the world, traditions are different. In Japan, people bow when they greet each other. In Italy, people celebrate the New Year with fireworks and wear red. In Mexico, the family makes a special dinner on the Day of the Dead. Traditions are beautiful because they are different — that is what makes the world interesting.
