Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Present Perfect: have/has + past participle for experiences (ever/never)
'have/has + fe'l' — tajriba haqida
I have downloaded that app.O'sha ilovani yuklab olganman.
She has never used a drone.U hech qachon dron ishlatmagan.
Have you ever searched for a recipe online?Internetdan retsept qidirganmisiz?
❌
Wrong: *I have download it.*
Right: *I have downloaded it.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 My Phone and Me

My phone is my best friend — but sometimes it's a problem. I have used it for three years, and I have downloaded hundreds of apps. I have never lost it, thank goodness! I use it to search for information, chat with friends and take photos. But last week I looked at the screen for five hours! Now I have a new rule: no phone after nine o'clock. Technology is amazing, but I am the boss, not my phone.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How long has the writer used his phone?
  2. What does he use it for?
  3. What new rule did he make?
  4. What does 'I am the boss' mean?

Listening Time!

NEW
🎧 Two Friends on the Phone

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What has the girl downloaded?
  2. What has the boy done this week?
  3. What does the girl advise?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Girl: 'I have downloaded a new language app. It's great!' Boy: 'Nice! I have watched five English videos this week.' Girl: 'Wow! I have never watched English videos.' Boy: 'You should try. Have you ever tried video calling?' Girl: 'Yes, I have. It's fun!'
