Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Problems → solutions: The problem is... / The best solution is to... / You should... / If I were you...
Muammo → yechim iboralari
The problem is I have no time.Muammo — vaqtim yo'q.
The best solution is to make a plan.Eng yaxshi yechim — reja tuzish.
If I were you, I would ask for help.Sizning o'rningizda bo'lsam, yordam so'rardim.
❌
Wrong: *The solution is make a plan.*
Right: *The solution is to make a plan.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Crowded Bus Problem

Our bus is always crowded in the morning, and students are late. What is the solution? We discussed it in class. 'The problem is that there are too many students and too few buses,' said Akmal. 'The best solution is to add two more buses,' said Malika. 'Or we could start school ten minutes later,' suggested Timur. 'But that changes everything!' someone said. Our teacher smiled: 'Problems have many solutions. We need to choose the most practical one.' We wrote a letter to the bus company — and they answered!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What is the problem?
  2. What solution did Malika suggest?
  3. What did Timur suggest?
  4. What did the class do in the end?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 The Lost Phone

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What is the woman's problem?
  2. What would the man do?
  3. What should she do first?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Woman: 'I can't find my phone!' Man: 'Where did you last see it?' Woman: 'In the kitchen, I think.' Man: 'If I were you, I would call it first. Then check the kitchen — maybe it's under a book.' Woman: 'Good idea. And if I don't find it?' Man: 'The best solution is to use a tracking app. You should set one up!'
