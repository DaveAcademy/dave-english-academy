Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Feelings with am/is/are + adjective · feel + adjective · reasons with because
'am/is/are' + sifat; sabab — 'because'
I am happy because I passed the test.Testdan o'tganim uchun xursandman.
She feels nervous before exams.U imtihondan oldin asabiy bo'ladi.
They were excited about the trip.Ular safardan hayajonlandi.
❌
Wrong: *I am very happy because of I...*
Right: *I am happy because I...*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 A Big Surprise

Today was an exciting day. In the morning, I felt nervous because we had a maths test. I studied hard all week, so I was calm. After the test, my teacher smiled and said: 'Well done!' I felt proud and happy. When I came home, my family was waiting for me. 'Surprise!' they shouted — they had prepared my favourite dinner. I was so surprised and happy. It was a perfect day.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Why was the writer nervous in the morning?
  2. How did he feel after the test?
  3. What did the family prepare?
  4. How did he feel at the end?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 How Do They Feel?

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Why is Lola sad?
  2. Why is Umid angry?
  3. Why is Bobur excited?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Lola is sad because her best friend moved to another city. Umid is angry because his brother broke his new phone. Bobur is excited because tomorrow he is going on a trip with his class. Everyone has different feelings today.
