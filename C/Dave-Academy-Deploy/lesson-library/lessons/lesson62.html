Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Travel expressions: It takes + time · depart/arrive at + time · by plane/train/bus
'It takes' + vaqt, 'by' + transport
It takes two hours by plane.Samolyotda ikki soat ketadi.
The train departs at 8:30.Poyezd 8:30 da jo'naydi.
By train, it takes six hours.Poyezdda olti soat ketadi.
❌
Wrong: *I go with bus.*
Right: *I go by bus.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 To Samarkand by Train

Last summer we travelled to Samarkand by train. We checked the timetable and bought tickets for the morning express. The train departed at 8:30 from platform 2. The journey took four hours. We watched the fields and mountains pass by the window. At noon we arrived at Samarkand station. The city was waiting for us — blue domes, kind people, and delicious bread.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How did the family travel to Samarkand?
  2. When did the train depart?
  3. How long did the journey take?
  4. What did they see from the window?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 At the Station

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Where is the woman going?
  2. When does her train depart?
  3. Which platform is it on?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. 'Excuse me, when does the next train to Bukhara depart?' 'At 2:15, from platform 4.' 'How long does it take?' 'About five hours.' 'One ticket, please.' 'Here you are. The train arrives in Bukhara at 7:20 in the evening.'
