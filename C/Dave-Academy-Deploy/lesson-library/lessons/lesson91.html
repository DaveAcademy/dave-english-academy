Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Describe a person: was born in / became / won / is famous for + -ing or noun
Shaxsni tasvirlash: 'was born, became, is famous for'
He was born in 1954 and became a writer.U 1954 yilda tug'ilgan va yozuvchi bo'lgan.
She is famous for winning gold medals.U oltin medallar yutgani bilan mashhur.
I admire her because she helps children.U bolalarga yordam bergani uchun qoyil qolaman.
❌
Wrong: *She famous for her work.*
Right: *She is famous for her work.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. He ___ born in 1980. (was/is)
  2. She is ___ for her films. (famous/fame)
  3. I ___ her hard work. (admire/admiring)
  4. He ___ a gold medal. (won/wins)

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Legend of Our School

Everyone at our school knows the name Bakhrom Xalilov. He was born in 1968 in a small village and walked four kilometres to school every day. He loved mathematics and worked hard. After school, he studied at university and became a famous engineer. He invented a cheap water pump for farmers — thousands of villages use it today. 'He is our school's legend,' our principal says. 'He shows us that a village boy can change the world.' I admire him because he never forgot where he came from.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. How far did Bakhrom walk to school?
  2. What did he study?
  3. What did he invent?
  4. Why does the writer admire him?

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What sport does the athlete do?
  2. When did she win her first medal?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Our guest today is a famous swimmer. 'I was born in 2001 and started swimming at five. I won my first medal at twelve. My parents supported me always.' 'What is your secret?' 'Training every day, even when I don't want to. Dreams need work.'
