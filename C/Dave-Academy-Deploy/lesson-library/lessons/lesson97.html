Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Second Conditional: If I were you, I would + verb (imaginary advice)
'If I were you, I would + fe'l' — maslahat
If I were you, I would rest more.Sizning o'rningizda bo'lsam, ko'proq dam olardim.
If I were you, I wouldn't worry.Sizning o'rningizda bo'lsam, xavotirlanmasdim.
Maybe you should talk to your teacher.Balki o'qituvchingiz bilan gaplashishingiz kerak.
❌
Wrong: *If I was you.*
Right: *If I were you.*

Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. If I ___ you, I would rest. (were/was)
  2. If I were you, I ___ talk to her. (would/will)
  3. I would ___ to the teacher. (talk/talking)
  4. Maybe you ___ ask. (should/shouldn't)

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Ask the Teacher

A student wrote to the school advice corner: 'I am always tired. I can't finish my homework, and I am worried about my exams.' The teacher replied: 'Thank you for your letter. If I were you, I would make a plan. Do the most important homework first. I would also sleep eight hours — a tired brain can't learn. Maybe you should rest between lessons instead of looking at your phone. If you try this for two weeks, you will see a difference. Good luck!'

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What is the student's problem?
  2. What should she do first, says the teacher?
  3. Why is sleep important?
  4. What should she do between lessons?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Advice for a Friend

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What is the boy worried about?
  2. What would the girl do if she were him?
  3. What should he do before the test?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Boy: 'I have a big test tomorrow and I'm worried.' Girl: 'If I were you, I wouldn't worry too much. You studied hard.' Boy: 'But I forget everything!' Girl: 'Maybe you should sleep well tonight. A rested brain remembers better.' Boy: 'You're right. I would sleep now.' Girl: 'Good idea. Good luck tomorrow!'
