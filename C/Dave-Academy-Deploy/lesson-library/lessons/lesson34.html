Grammar Time!

Grammatika
in, on, under, next to, between, behind, in front of + the + place
Joy so'zlari: ichida, ustida, tagida, yonida, orasida, orqasida, oldida
The lamp is on the desk.Chiroq parta ustida.
The ball is under the chair.To'p stul tagida.
The school is between the bank and the shop.Maktab bank va do'kon orasida.
❌
Wrong: *The cat is under of the table.*
Right: *The cat is under the table.*

  2. The shoes are ___ the bed. (under/above)
  3. The bank is ___ the shop and the hospital. (between/next to)

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti

📖 Where Is Everything?

It is a busy morning. Dad asks: 'Where is my phone?' Mum says: 'It's on the table, next to the coffee.' 'And my keys?' 'They're under the newspaper!' says Mum. Brother asks: 'Where are my shoes?' 'They're behind the door,' says Mum. 'And the cat?' The cat is on the sofa — sleeping, of course!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where is Dad's phone?
  2. Where are the keys?
  3. Where are the shoes?
  4. Where is the cat?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Listen and Place

🎵 4 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. Where is the pencil first?
  2. Where is the eraser?
  3. Where do you put the pencil next?
  4. Where is the eraser last?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Take a pencil and an eraser. Put the pencil on the book. Put the eraser under the pencil. Put the pencil next to the cup. Put the eraser between the book and the phone.

❓ Mini Quiz
  1. Translate: 'Qalam kitob ustida.' → __
  2. The cat is ___ the table. (under/on — hidden below)
  3. The school is ___ the bank and the shop. (between/in)

Vocabulary: 10 position words 3× · Grammar: 6 sentences with different prepositions about your room · Reading: re-read 'Where Is Everything?', answer 4 questions · Speaking: hide an object at home and ask your family to find it.

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Prepositions — Lesson 34

  • Make the prepositions physical: move a pencil as you drill each word.
  • Review L30-33 furniture/rooms so students have rich vocabulary to position.
  • Watch for 'under of' — drill 'under the table' chorally.
  • The listening task is Simon-says style — students follow with real objects.
