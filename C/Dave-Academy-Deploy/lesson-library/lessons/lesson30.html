Grammar Time!

Grammatika
There is a + one thing / There are + two or more
Bitta narsa uchun 'there is', ko'pi uchun 'there are'
There is a bed in my room.Mening xonamda to'shak bor.
There are three chairs in the kitchen.Oshxonada uchta stul bor.
This is my desk. That is the wardrobe.Bu mening partam. Anavi — shkaf.
❌
Wrong: *There is two lamps.*
Right: *There are two lamps.*

Let's Practice!

Mashqlar
CORE
Fill in the Gaps(Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring)
  1. There ___ a sofa in the living room. (is/are)
  2. There ___ four chairs at the table. (is/are)
  3. ___ is my mirror — point to the near one. (This/That)
  4. ___ are two lamps — point far away. (These/Those)
Match the Pairs(Moslang)
EnglishUzbek
WardrobeShkaf

Circle the Answer(To'g'ri javobni belgilang)
  1. What's this? → a) It's a lamp b) It's a sofa c) It's a bed
  2. What are these? → a) It's a chair b) They are chairs c) They is chairs
Build the Sentence(So'zlarni joylashtiring)
  1. There / a / is / mirror / wall / on / the / .
  2. There / are / chairs / two / the / in / room / .

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti

📖 My Dream Room

My dream room is big. There is a bed next to the window. There are two lamps — one on the desk and one next to the bed. There is a big wardrobe and a soft carpet on the floor. On the wall there is a mirror and three pictures. There is a sofa too. I love my room!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. Where is the bed?
  2. How many lamps are there?
  3. What is on the floor?
  4. What is on the wall?

Teacher Notes

O'qituvchi uchun

📚 Furniture — Lesson 30

  • Show a picture of a room; students name the furniture in English.
  • Drill 'This/That' with real objects — near/far hand gestures.
  • Watch for 'there is + plural'; contrast 'There is a chair / There are chairs'.
  • Follow up: each student describes their own room at home.
