Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Frequency adverb goes before the main verb (I always do) but after am/is/are (I am always...)
Ravish fe'ldan oldin, 'am/is/are' dan keyin keladi
I always do my homework.Har doim uy vazifasini qilaman.
She is always happy.U har doim xursand.
We sometimes go to the cinema.Ba'zan kinoga boramiz.
❌
Wrong: *I do always my homework.*
Right: *I always do my homework.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 My Morning Routine

I have a simple morning routine. I always get up at six o'clock and I am always on time for school. I usually have bread and tea for breakfast. I often walk to school with my friend. I sometimes listen to music on the way. I rarely miss a day. I never go to school without my books — that is my golden rule!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What time does the writer get up?
  2. What does he usually have for breakfast?
  3. How does he go to school?
  4. What is his golden rule?
