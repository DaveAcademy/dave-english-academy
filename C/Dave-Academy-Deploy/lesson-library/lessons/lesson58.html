Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Describe a week: On + day + activity + frequency adverb
'On' + kun + mashg'ulot + ravish
On Monday I always go to school.Dushanba kuni har doim maktabga boraman.
On Friday I sometimes go to the club.Juma kuni ba'zan to'garakka boraman.
At the weekend I usually rest.Dam olish kunlari odatda dam olaman.
❌
Wrong: *In Monday I go.*
Right: *On Monday I go.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 Aziz's Week

Aziz has a busy but happy week. On Monday he goes to school and always does his homework. On Tuesday he plays football with his friends. On Wednesday he usually helps his mother in the kitchen. On Thursday he reads a book. On Friday he sometimes goes to the cooking club. At the weekend, he plays with his cousins and rests. A typical week for Aziz!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What does Aziz always do on Monday?
  2. What does he do on Tuesday?
  3. Where does he go on Fridays?
  4. What does he do at the weekend?
