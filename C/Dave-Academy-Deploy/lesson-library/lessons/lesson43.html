Grammar Time!

Grammatika
some (positive) / any (negative & questions) · countable: a/an, -s · uncountable: no plural
'some' — tasdiqda, 'any' — inkor va savolda
There is some milk.Sut bor.
There aren't any eggs.Tuxum yo'q.
Are there any apples?Olmalar bormi?
❌
Wrong: *I have any bread.*
Right: *I have some bread.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The Shopping List

Mother made a shopping list. 'We have some rice and sugar, but there isn't any bread. There aren't any eggs, and there isn't any milk.' Aziz took the list and went to the shop. He bought some bread, a packet of eggs and a bottle of milk. He also bought some chocolate for himself — but he didn't tell mother!

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What food is in the house?
  2. What did Aziz buy?
  3. What did Aziz buy for himself?

Teacher Notes

📚 Food and Quantities — Lesson 43

  • Use real or drawn food items to make some/any physical.
  • Explain countable (can count: eggs, apples) vs uncountable (rice, water).
  • Watch for 'some' in negative sentences.
  • Shopping-list activity is a great model for real life.
