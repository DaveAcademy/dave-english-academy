Grammar Time!

Grammatika
Suggestions: should + verb · We must + verb for strong obligation · problem → solution
Taklif: 'should', qat'iy — 'must'
We should recycle more.Ko'proq qayta ishlashimiz kerak.
People must stop throwing rubbish in the river.Odamlar daryoga axlat tashlashni to'xtatishi kerak.
Don't waste water.Suvni isrof qilmang.
❌
Wrong: *We should to recycle.*
Right: *We should recycle.*

Reading Time!

O'qish vaqti
📖 The River That Was Clean

Twenty years ago, the river near our town was clean. People swam in it and caught fish. Then factories came. They threw waste into the water, and pollution grew. The fish disappeared. Our class decided to help. We organized a cleaning day — students, teachers and parents collected rubbish from the banks. We also talked to the factory. Now it cleans its water. 'Small actions change big problems,' our teacher said. The river is slowly coming back.

❓ Comprehension Questions (Matn savollari)
  1. What was the river like twenty years ago?
  2. What happened when factories came?
  3. What did the class organize?
  4. What did they do about the factory?

Listening Time!

Tinglash vaqti
NEW
🎧 Eco Tips

🎵 3 tasks — listen and answer.

  1. What should the family do with plastic bottles?
  2. How should they save energy?
  3. What advice about shopping?

🔧 Script (teacher reads aloud)

Listen. Today's eco tips. First, you should recycle plastic bottles — don't throw them in the bin. Second, turn off the lights when you leave a room; it saves energy. Third, take a cloth bag to the market instead of plastic bags. And remember: don't waste water while brushing your teeth. Small steps, big change!
