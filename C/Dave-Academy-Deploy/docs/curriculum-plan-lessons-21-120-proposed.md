# Proposed Curriculum Plan — 100-Lesson Continuous Curriculum

**Status: PLANNING DOCUMENT ONLY.** Nothing here has been written to
`curriculum_lessons`. No migrations, no PDFs. For Dave's review and approval
before any rows are inserted.

**Supersedes the earlier 120-lesson draft with reserved Review/Test slots.**
Per Dave's revised rule: the curriculum is now exactly 100 lessons, every
number 1–100 is a real teaching lesson (workshop/speaking-circle lessons
count as real lessons), and there are no reserved/empty Review or Test rows
or gaps. Reviews/tests/quizzes are teacher-scheduled events, not curriculum
lessons.

## Constraints honored

- **Already-published lessons never renumbered:** 1–8, 11–18 (real content,
  PDFs uploaded, live) keep their exact numbers and content unchanged.
- **Existing but unbuilt curriculum anchors kept at their numbers:** 34
  (Prepositions), 44 (How Many/How Much), 56 (My Weekend), 61 (Holidays and
  Celebrations), 71 (Body Parts), 72 (Health Problems), 73 (At the Doctor's).
- **9, 10, 19, 20** — previously empty Review/Test placeholders — become 4
  new real Foundation-band lessons, closing every gap in 1–20.
- **21–100 (minus the anchors above)** are newly designed, continuous, no
  gaps.
- Workshop and speaking-circle lessons are real numbered curriculum lessons
  (matching how Lessons 7, 8, 17, 18 already work) — they consolidate
  through activity/speaking rather than introducing lots of new content, but
  they are not empty placeholders.

## Bands (per Dave's revised roadmap)

| Lessons | Band | CEFR |
|---|---|---|
| 1–20 | Foundation | A0 → A1 |
| 21–40 | Elementary | A1 |
| 41–60 | Pre-Intermediate | Strong A1 |
| 61–80 | Strong A2 | A1+ / Early A2 → A2 |
| 81–100 | Beginning B1 | A2 → Strong A2/B1 |
